## What a real facility does (the research, in one page)

**Movement is a corridor with gates in it.** A campus prison has a main
corridor ("main street") connecting the housing units, the chow hall, medical,
education, industries and visiting. It is cut into sections by sliding barred
grille gates; a section is opened for a movement period and shut behind it.
Where a section meets a secure zone there is a **sally port / interlock**: two
gates in series, the second does not open until the first has shut. That is
the only mechanism the whole building has, repeated
([Fentress, the sally port](https://blog.fentress.com/blog/sallyport/),
[Sally port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sally_port),
[corridor and sally-port lighting](https://kenall.com/Home/Applications/Correctional/Corridors-Sally-Ports)).

**Keys are few, and they are on rings.** An officer draws one issued key ring
for his post at the start of a shift; rings are never split; restricted keys
(perimeter, armoury) are issued only to designated people; emergency sets sit
in the control centre; everything is counted every shift
([ICE key and lock control standard](https://www.ice.gov/doclib/dro/detention-standards/pdf/key_and_lock_control.pdf),
[Oregon DOC 40.1.2 keys and locks](https://www.oregon.gov/doc/rules-and-policies/Documents/40-1-2-keys-and-locks.pdf),
[key control in corrections](https://www.morsewatchmans.com/markets/corrections)).
So a corridor is not forty keys; it is ONE ring on the movement officer, and
the perimeter is ONE restricted set.

**Towers are reached by a ladder or a stair with a hatch, and have a rail.**
([prefab guard towers](https://www.panelbuilt.com/products/guard-towers/),
[portable guard tower access options](https://perimetersecurityproducts.com/products/portable-guard-tower/)).

## The key scheme — five keys total, one of them new

| key | opens | where it is |
|---|---|---|
| Keycard (exists) | electric doors: yard door, the four sally gates, the booths | duty desk, the officer whose post is the door |
| Cell Key (exists) | your own cell at night | a screw's belt |
| Gun-Room Key (exists) | the armoury cage, central control | the warden |
| **Corridor Key (new)** | **every grille in the corridor network** | the two movement officers walking the spine; the board in central control |
| Gate Key (exists since 2026-09-05) | the perimeter sally ports (all three) | the gate detail; the gate booth |

Electric locks answer the control room's throw; grilles and perimeter gates are
manual and do not. C4 opens anything; a lockpick opens cages.

## The network

Spine (4.5 m wide, block-lined, roofed, lit 24 h): west leg x=-40 from the
north spine to z=44 then x=-50 to z=118; north leg z=-72 from x=-40 to x=44
(the service yard's gate); east leg x=40 to z=44 then x=50 to z=118. Wings:
to industries (z=22), the powerhouse (z=81), segregation (z=21.5), the kitchen
(z=81), visitation (z=115), central control (a stub at x=0), the rec yard
(x=-48). Interlocks at the four yard sally gates (card, then grille). A grille
every 35-40 m and at each wing. Three perimeter sally ports: the main one at
(0,128) off the south block, and two at the ends of the spine (±50,128).

Escape reads: yard → card → interlock → corridor key → a long walk with guards
in it → gate key → out. Or the crawls, the culvert, C4, the roof.

## Population

The population of this prison is what it can sleep (prisonBeds), and the wing's
72 beds were full, so the ring had nobody. Housing Unit B (24 double bunks off
the north spine) adds 48. Named rigs, which muster on foot, spawn only where they
can walk to a bunk without a grille in the way: the two yards, Unit B and the
corridor pocket outside it. The ambient crowd, which travels by portal, takes the
ring's rooms as islands: the rec yard, industries, the kitchen, visitation, the
spine legs, Unit B. A posted officer stands in every ring building, four
movement officers walk the spine's legs carrying the Corridor Key and opening the
grilles as they go (a following man's window), the perimeter patrol walks the
road.

## Accessibility

Every tower's ladder is a climb (the vent mechanism: stand at the foot, press,
you are on the deck) and the deck has a rail collider. Gun racks are solid.
