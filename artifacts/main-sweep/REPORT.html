The one thing that looked like a regression

Bot kills on the gate seed fell from 301 to 200. That is either roll variance or a quiet nerf — and a nerf would be the exact opposite of what you asked for, so it was worth resolving before committing rather than after.

It is variance. Across three further seeds the totals are 617 (new) against 664 (old) — about 7%, well inside the spread a per-occurrence roll produces. The reason the first seed looked so stark is below.

The quake, specifically

The old game handed you an M7.4 every single time

The legacy quake had one fixed destructiveness — collapseFrac 0.3. Solving the new magnitude model for that same value gives M7.4. That was every earthquake the game had ever produced, on every seed, in every round. It is a precise measurement of the thing you complained about.

The replacement rolls M4.0–M8.6, with a real damage threshold: below about M5.2 a quake damages nothing, which is true of actual earthquakes. The round’s escalating intensity skews the draw:

The gate only ever samples an early round. That is the whole explanation for its quake going from 49 kills to 9.

The bug found on the way

The flash flood’s hydrograph ran backwards

The comment in disasters.js has always claimed the water “goes UP fast and out slowly, which is what makes the drain the part that strands you.” The numbers underneath it did the opposite: rise 0.42 of the window, stand 0.26, drain 0.32. The flood took longer to arrive than to leave — a bathtub filling and emptying, which cancelled the one thing the event exists to teach.

The preset had to move with it

flashflood-stages.mjs pins each beat to an absolute sim-second, so the correction silently invalidated all five. “The lake at full depth” at 12.4 s is a puddle under the real curve, and “two feet floats a car” at 8.2 s no longer clears the 0.6 m threshold it needs to fire at all.

Retimed: front 3.4→1.8, knockdown 5.6→3.6, lake 12.4→4.4, car-adrift 8.2→5.6, drain 16.6→11.0. atSecs only ever steps forward, so lake now precedes car-adrift and that constraint is written into the file instead of being rediscovered.

The correction

The cabinet already exists — outside this repo

I first reported before-after-tool-arch-i72lov as the one branch worth rescuing: 6,072 additive lines, merges clean, touches no game file. That was wrong, and the owner caught it in a sentence — “if it’s ancient code you might be looking at the wrong shit.”

~/harness is its own git repository, begun 2026-08-27 — a week after that branch — and it is committed to nightly. Its log is the branch’s own goals, delivered:

What the branch was buildingWhat ~/harness already shipped
ba/lib/store.mjs — durable receipt archive“The cabinet is one ledger: artifacts belong to the session that made them”
ba/lib/show.mjs — receipt renderer“A receipt is not an empty thumbnail: show the facts it carries”
ba/lib/adopt.mjs, agents.mjs“The Library: 591 closed sessions, and a way to take them”

It also lives in the right place. Machine-wide tooling belongs to the machine, not inside one game — which is exactly what ~/harness/AGENTS.md says it is. Merging the branch would have planted a second, older copy of the cabinet inside gta6.
