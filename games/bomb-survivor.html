BOMB SURVIVOR

BOMB THE CITY. THEN SURVIVE IT.

  1. 1First half you fly a bomber. Flak guns in the streets shoot back. Knock them out, then hunt their crews. Fly low to hit, high to live.
  2. 2Second half you are on foot in the same city. Run to a flak gun, man it and shoot the bombers down.
  3. 3A red ring on the ground is where a bomb will land. Get out of it, or get under a roof.
