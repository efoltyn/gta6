THE ACCOUNTING

Reclaimed. The B-2 (city/strategic.js), the fighter, helicopter, tank, truck and transport (city/island_military.js), the people (entities/character.js + CBZ.animChar) and the park trees (world/vegetation.js) are all the shipped models now. The change that made it possible was small and belongs to the engine: those files were welded to the archipelago only at their REGISTRATION, not at their geometry, so each now publishes its model factories (CBZ.milModels, CBZ.strategicModels) and guards its CBZ.addLandmass call the way marina.js and bank.js already did. Nothing about the shipped game changed — the base below those exports still calls the same local functions.
